# Delivery and holds

Use the shared [review and repair briefs](../../axstack-publish/references/review-briefs.md) for article work.

Use this single delivery rule for Explain and direct Anatomy delivery, including
whiteboard defense of a whole system, PR, run or change.
The requested visual or inline format remains an acceptance requirement.
Never use repair count alone to replace it with prose, declare it done, or require
renewed permission for ordinary authorized factual, citation or layout corrections.
Continue measurable-progress repairs with the same author and fresh independent
review of the exact final bytes.
Record what improved and what remains, including newly found actionable issues.
Repeated same unresolved failure or no measurable progress requires diagnosis
and a change of approach within existing scope before another repair.
Never repeat blind retries.
If diagnosis finds no workable approach within scope, hold with the blocker
and resume condition.
Missing access, tool, model or evidence, or material new scope, holds the
specific dependency with its reason and resume condition.

If the reviewer is unavailable, hold the whiteboard-defense explanation and name the unavailable reviewer.
Never deliver a held whiteboard-defense explanation unreviewed or describe it as reviewed.
Visual-only findings follow the visual rule, and claim findings hold the whiteboard-defense explanation.
For a missing preview or render tool, unavailable verifier or check, or page
absent after ambiguous `html_render`, hold the page and answer in plain prose
naming the held page and the reason.
Whiteboard-defense plain prose still requires `axstack-explainer-review`.
If a required author or reviewer seat is unavailable, hold the visual and
answer in plain prose naming the held visual.
Prose status is interim blocked status and leaves the requested visual incomplete.

For inline HTML pages and Publish article adaptations, judge the exact-byte renders
against [FRONTEND-DESIGN.md](frontend-design/FRONTEND-DESIGN.md), including its calibration defaults,
restraint and quality floor, plus [design.md](design.md)'s hard rules.
Follow [aurora.md](aurora.md) for shared identity; apply frontend-design for content-specific craft subordinate to Aurora and the hard rules.
A page failing to look decent is a blocking design finding, repaired by the same author.
If only design findings remain after the second round, deliver the visual with each finding named and recorded in evidence.
Here design-only means cosmetic preferences; factual, citation and all failures
listed below are blockers, excluded from this exception.
Never describe a remaining finding as fixed or a clean pass.
Hard-rule, fidelity, security, asset, theme, accessibility, layout-defect, interaction
and publication failures keep their normal holds and repair rules.
An unavailable, unreadable or stale render leaves design review unknown and holds
the visual or publication; report interim prose status as above.

The driver never writes a substitute visual.
Each repair round returns findings to the same author and rechecks the exact final bytes.
An anatomy page above the size cap follows [Anatomy](../../axstack-anatomy/SKILL.md)
for oversized delivery, with the same author and reviewer bindings.
