# Architecture diagrams

Apply this mode to diagram requests about how parts fit together, who talks to
whom, and where risks sit. Ordinary diagrams keep Explain's existing path.
This mode specializes authorship and review for architecture/system maps in any
requested format; Explain still owns its 700-word cap, claim labels and delivery.
Whole-work defense also follows the linked whiteboard-defense route in Explain §2.

## 1. Bound and ground

The driver first bounds the question under Explain §1.
After the driver bounds the question, require `axstack-scout` grounding before
research or authorship. Follow [Scout](../../axstack-scout/SKILL.md) for its
content identity, required seats and evidence reconciliation.
Reuse scout evidence only when its inspected content identity is unchanged.

After grounding, require `axstack-research` only when the diagram depends on
external, vendor or platform facts outside the inspected repositories.
Otherwise the driver records `not needed (<reason>)` for research.
When research is needed, follow [Research](../../axstack-research/SKILL.md) for
its source standards, configured branches and required checks.
Reconcile scout and research evidence before author dispatch.
Resolve conflicting material claims or label them unknown before design.

The driver keeps the source map outside the reader view.
Follow [Fidelity](fidelity.md) when mapping nodes and edges to evidence and
marking planned or unknown elements; include supporting evidence for badges,
markers and legend claims in the map as well.

## 2. Create with one author

Before dispatch, follow the [T3 runtime boundary](../../axstack/references/t3-runtime.md)
and [Role roster](../../axstack/references/role-roster.md) for the selected
preset's actual bindings and availability.
Give `axstack-explainer` the reconciled evidence and source map in its author brief.
Include the bounded question, audience, format, inspected identities and open gaps.
Require `axstack-explainer` as the one writer of the architecture diagram.
The driver never authors architecture diagrams.
Return every repair to the same author.
The author follows Explain's existing format and design references.

## 3. Review the final artifact

After authorship and before delivery, require independent
`axstack-explainer-review` on the exact final bytes.
Require the reviewer provider to be different from the actual author provider,
using session provenance. Record the actual author and reviewer session,
provider and model alongside the artifact identity; role names alone prove
neither provenance nor independence.
Review the full claim set every round, including nodes, edges, badges and
markers, legend and prior findings.
Give the reviewer the source map and reconciled evidence so it can reopen the
sources independently, rather than trusting the author's account.

For inline HTML, the driver captures 728px dark and 360px light previews before
review, bound to SHA-256. The reviewer inspects the driver's 728px dark and
360px light previews for design review.
Follow [Inline pages](inline-pages.md) for exact-byte scans, preview acceptance
and any interaction verification, and [Visual QA](visual-qa.md) for reviewer
design checks and other requested artifact formats.
Every byte change requires fresh review.

## 4. Deliver or hold

The driver renders only reviewed bytes after the review passes.
Follow [Delivery](delivery.md) for repairs, missing evidence or tools, and
delivery readback; interim prose leaves the requested diagram incomplete.
If a required seat is unavailable or rejected, hold the diagram and name the
blocker and resume condition.
Never substitute a required seat, provider or model.
Single-provider presets hold for a user routing choice.
This mode never permits same-provider author and review through Explain's
session-independence exception.
